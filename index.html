Detection Methodology
Core Question
How justified is a posture change for this service region — right now?
Scoring Pipeline
Base signal score      (event type × source confidence)
+ Spatial relevance    (intersects or near operating region)
+ Corroboration       (second independent source confirms)
+ Persistence         (condition active across refresh cycles)
− Telemetry gap       (no secondary validation available)
− Temporal decay      (stale signal, no new activity)
──────────────────────────────
= Operational relevance score
Posture Thresholds
≥ 90 → SUSPEND   Conditions exceed safe tolerance
≥ 75 → RESTRICT  Routing/dispatch modifications needed
≥ 45 → WATCH     Degrading — increased awareness
≥ 22 → MONITOR   Emerging — low confidence signal
< 22 → NORMAL    No operationally relevant condition
Suppression Logic
Signals are suppressed if they score below threshold, have no spatial intersection with any service region, are exact duplicates, are context-only probabilistic outlooks without corroboration, or decay after 4+ refresh cycles without resolution. Suppression is a feature. The most important capability is correctly deciding NOT to escalate.
Confidence ≠ Severity
A high score means the escalation is well-justified — not that conditions are dangerous. You can have high confidence in a moderate condition, or low confidence in a potentially severe one. The score answers: how much evidence supports changing operational posture?
Telemetry Layer
This prototype uses public environmental signals only. In production, fleet telemetry — reroute frequency, speed degradation, remote assistance volume, perception health, hard braking clusters — would serve as the primary corroboration layer, increasing score confidence and reducing false positives.
